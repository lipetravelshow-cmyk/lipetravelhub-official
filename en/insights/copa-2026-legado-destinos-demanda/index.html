After the Final Whistle: The 2026 World Cup Legacy Depends on What Destinations Do Next is best read as a market signal rather than a standalone trend. The point is to understand how the shift changes demand, distribution, product design and the quality of decisions across the travel ecosystem.

Strategic lens

Destination performance is no longer explained by arrivals alone. Connectivity, positioning, product depth, friction, perceived value and the ability to disperse demand now shape competitiveness.

The strategic value is not in naming the trend. It is in understanding what it changes — and acting before the new behavior becomes obvious to everyone.

What this changes for decision-makers

The practical agenda is to move from visibility to value capture: make the destination easier to understand, easier to buy and more distinctive in the moments that influence choice.

What to watch now

Watch the gap between volume and value, the role of air access and distribution, and how destinations translate cultural or lifestyle appeal into bookable product.
